## Slide 6: Milestone 2 — Biometric Palm Vision AI
### MediaPipe 3D Landmark Tracking & Bézier Morphometry
* **Google MediaPipe Hand Mesh**:
  * Extracts **21 normalized 3D anatomical landmarks** (wrist, metacarpal joints, PIP/DIP joints, and fingertips).
  * Dual capture modes: high-resolution static photo upload (`.jpg`, `.png`) or real-time live webcam video streaming.
* **Quadratic Bézier Line Curve Morphometry**:
  * Computes arc lengths along anatomical anchor points for the 4 primary palm lines:
    $$\text{Arc Length} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \|\mathbf{p}_i - \mathbf{p}_{i-1}\|$$
  * Measures **Heart Line** (emotional resonance), **Head Line** (intellectual focus), **Life Line** (vitality & stamina), and **Fate Line** (career direction).
* **Relative Per-Hand Ratio Calibration**:
  * Solved camera-distance variance by normalizing line lengths against the hand's own palm aspect ratio ($\text{width} / \text{height}$) and finger-to-palm ratio:
    $$\text{Baseline} = 75 + 10 \times (\text{aspect\_ratio} - 1.15) + 10 \times (\text{finger\_ratio} - 0.9)$$
* **Gesture & Mount Recognition**: Identifies Mudras, Open Palm, and surface curvature across planetary mounts (Jupiter, Saturn, Apollo, Venus, Luna).

> **Speaker Notes / Talking Points:**

## Slide 12: Engineering Challenges & Innovative Solutions
### Key Technical Obstacles Overcome
1. **Challenge: Camera Distance & Scale Invariance in Palmistry**
   * *Problem*: Users upload images taken from varying camera distances, corrupting raw pixel line lengths.
   * *Solution*: Formulated an adaptive baseline taking into account the hand's own measured palm aspect ratio and finger-palm ratio, making curve scores invariant to camera zoom.
2. **Challenge: Astronomical Accuracy in Astrology**
   * *Problem*: Standard astronomical packages compute Western tropical zodiacs rather than Vedic sidereal positions.
   * *Solution*: Embedded `pyswisseph` with `FLG_SIDEREAL` and Lahiri ayanamsa modes, computing Julian Day UT conversions and true local house cusps.
3. **Challenge: Preventing AI Hallucinations in Guidance**
   * *Problem*: Unconstrained LLMs generate arbitrary predictions disconnected from traditional cards or lines.
   * *Solution*: Engineered a domain-specific in-memory TF-IDF knowledge base that retrieves validated excerpts to bound and ground all generative text.
4. **Challenge: Real-Time Vision Processing Latency**
   * *Problem*: Executing deep computer vision models during HTTP requests risked blocking the FastAPI event loop.
   * *Solution*: Offloaded image processing into FastAPI thread pools (`run_in_threadpool`) and optimized OpenCV pre-scaling for sub-second responses.

> **Speaker Notes / Talking Points:**
> "Throughout the project, I tackled several tough engineering challenges. I overcame palm image scaling issues with relative ratio normalization, ensured arc-second astrological precision with Swiss Ephemeris sidereal flags, eliminated AI hallucinations with TF-IDF grounded RAG, and maintained sub-second vision response times using FastAPI thread pools."
