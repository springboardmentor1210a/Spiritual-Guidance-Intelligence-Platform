Engineering Rigor

Challenges Faced & Technical Solutions

Overcoming Real-World Computer Vision, Astrophysics, and Generative AI Hurdles

1. Palm Image Scale Variance

Challenge: Raw pixel line lengths fluctuated depending on camera distance and angle.

Solution: Invented relative Bézier scoring that normalizes lengths against the hand's own internal palm aspect ratio and finger proportions.

2. Astronomical Precision in Astrology

Challenge: Standard astro packages calculate Western tropical zodiacs rather than Vedic sidereal charts.

Solution: Integrated pyswisseph with explicit sidereal and Lahiri ayanamsa modes, computing Julian Day UT shifts and true house cusps.

3. Preventing AI Hallucinations

Challenge: Unconstrained LLMs often produce contradictory or invented spiritual claims.

Solution: Built an in-memory TF-IDF knowledge base that injects validated card and palmline definitions directly into the prompt context.

4. Vision Inference Latency

Challenge: Deep vision models on the backend risked blocking the FastAPI async event loop.

Solution: Offloaded image processing into FastAPI thread pools (run_in_threadpool) with OpenCV pre-scaling for sub-second responses.
